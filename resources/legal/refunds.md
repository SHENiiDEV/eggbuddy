This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when and how you can get a refund for purchases of coins in {{brand}}, a service operated by **{{company_name}}**. It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions]({{terms_url}}). Nothing in this Policy affects your statutory rights as a consumer.

## 1. What you buy

When you top up, you buy **coins**: virtual in-game currency that is digital content. Coins are delivered **instantly**: they are credited to your account as soon as your payment is confirmed. There are no physical goods, no shipping and no subscriptions.

## 2. Right of withdrawal for digital content

If you are a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a purchase of digital content. However, at checkout you **expressly ask us to supply the coins immediately** and **acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once the coins have been credited** to your account. For this reason the statutory right of withdrawal ends when the coins are delivered.

If, for any reason, coins from a purchase have not been delivered to your account, you keep your right to withdraw from that purchase and to receive a full refund.

## 3. When you are entitled to a refund

We will refund you in full when:

- **You were charged but did not receive the coins** and we cannot deliver them within a reasonable time after you contact us.
- **You were charged more than once** for the same purchase (a duplicate charge).
- **A payment was made without your authorisation** (for example, if your card was used by someone else). Please tell us and your bank immediately.
- **There was a technical error on our side**, for example the wrong amount was charged or the wrong number of coins was delivered.
- **We close your account or discontinue the Service** for reasons other than your breach of the Terms; in that case we refund purchased coins you have not used.
- **A refund is otherwise required by law.**

## 4. 14-day goodwill refunds

Even after your right of withdrawal has ended, we offer a goodwill refund if **all** of the following apply:

1. you ask for it within **14 days** of the purchase;
2. none of the coins from that purchase have been used, which means your current coin balance is at least the number of coins (including bonus coins) credited by that purchase; and
3. you have not received a goodwill refund in the previous 12 months.

When we make a goodwill refund, the coins from that purchase (including any bonus coins) are removed from your balance.

## 5. What we cannot refund

Except where sections 3 or 4 apply, or where the law requires otherwise, we cannot refund:

- coins that have already been used to unlock skins, treats or other digital items;
- digital items unlocked with coins;
- coins received for free, such as the welcome gift, bonus coins on their own or coins earned in the mini-game;
- purchases made more than 14 days ago where the coins were delivered correctly;
- in-game events, such as a pet becoming sick or leaving the game.

## 6. How to request a refund

Email [{{company_email}}](mailto:{{company_email}}) from the email address registered on your account, and include:

- your name and account email;
- the invoice number (you can find it in your receipt email and on your account page), or the date and amount of the payment;
- the reason for your request.

We will confirm receipt within **two (2) business days** and give you our decision within **five (5) business days**.

## 7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid **to the original payment method** (the card used for the purchase) in the original currency, for the full amount paid. We process approved refunds within **five (5) business days**, and in any case no later than 14 days after we agree to the refund. Depending on your card issuer, it may take a further **5–10 business days** for the money to appear on your statement. We do not charge any fee for refunds. When a refund is made, the coins from that purchase are removed from your balance.

## 8. Cancellations

- **Before payment:** you can cancel any purchase at any time before you confirm the payment, simply by closing the checkout page or pressing "Cancel". You will not be charged.
- **Subscriptions:** we do not sell subscriptions and we never charge your card on a recurring basis, so there is nothing to cancel after a purchase.
- **Closing your account:** you can close your account at any time from your [account page]({{account_url}}). Closing your account does not by itself give you a refund of unused coins, unless section 3 or 4 applies.

## 9. Chargebacks

If you see a charge you do not recognise or have a problem with a purchase, please contact us first: we can usually resolve it faster than your bank. Charges appear on your statement as **{{descriptor}}**. If you dispute a payment with your bank (a chargeback), the coins from that purchase will be removed and your account may be suspended while the dispute is investigated, as described in our [Terms & Conditions]({{terms_url}}).
