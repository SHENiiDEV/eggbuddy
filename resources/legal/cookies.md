## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They let the website remember things such as whether you are logged in. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, work in a similar way; in this Policy we call all of them "cookies".

Cookies set by the website you are visiting are "first-party" cookies. Cookies set by another company are "third-party" cookies.

## 2. How we use cookies

We keep cookies to a minimum. {{brand}} uses:

- **Strictly necessary cookies.** These are required for the website to work: to keep you logged in, to protect forms against cross-site request forgery, and to remember your cookie choices. They cannot be switched off. Under the law they do not require your consent.
- **Functional (preference) cookies, optional.** These remember choices you make, such as whether game sounds are on. We only use them if you allow them.

We do **not** use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use analytics cookies. If we ever introduce analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this Policy and ask for your consent first.

## 4. Payment provider cookies

When you top up, you may be redirected to our payment provider's secure payment page or to your bank's 3-D Secure page. Those websites may set their own cookies, for example for fraud prevention and to complete authentication. These cookies are controlled by those providers and are governed by their own cookie and privacy policies.

## 5. Managing your cookie choices

When you first visit the website, a banner lets you accept or refuse optional cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the website, such as logging in and making payments, will not work.
